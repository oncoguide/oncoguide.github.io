OncoGuide is a project built by a patient, for patients, run by Asociatia OncoGuide -- a registered Romanian non-profit. All content is and will remain free, with no ads.

If you want to help us keep this information alive and up to date, the simplest way from anywhere in the world is a direct bank transfer. The account details are below, in an EU bank, with the full IBAN and BIC for international transfers.

## Direct donation by bank transfer

Available to anyone -- individual or company, in Romania or abroad. The account details below are filled automatically from the association's official data. A transfer takes only a couple of minutes, and any amount helps.

## Where the money goes

Asociatia OncoGuide has a single statutory purpose: closing the gap between medical knowledge and the information available to cancer patients. Funds support keeping the site running, access to medical sources, and new guides in 6 languages.

For transparency: the association keeps a record of received donations, and our donor privacy policy is public: [donor privacy]({{< relref "/donate-privacy" >}}).

## How OncoGuide stays up to date

We use the latest state-of-the-art AI research tools to track everything new and relevant for each diagnosis we cover: therapies, clinical trials, and practice-changing evidence, explained in plain language.

Every published guide gets a periodic review -- roughly monthly -- so it stays current: if something new appears in the meantime, we want it on the site, not just in the journals.

**Do you have a specific diagnosis?** We are expanding coverage diagnosis by diagnosis. We will do our best to start tracking it on the site.
