OncoGuide is a project built by a patient, for patients, run by Asociatia OncoGuide -- a registered Romanian non-profit. All content is and will remain free, with no ads.

If you want to help us keep this information alive and up to date, the simplest way from anywhere in the world is a direct bank transfer -- the account details are below, in an EU bank (full IBAN and BIC for international transfers).

## Romanian taxpayers: two zero-cost options

If you pay taxes in Romania, you can support OncoGuide at zero personal cost by redirecting taxes you already owe:

<ul class="donate-paths">
<li>

[**Companies: sponsorship**]({{< relref "/donate/firme" >}})

Romanian companies paying profit tax can redirect up to **20% of their tax** to OncoGuide. The dedicated page (in Romanian) generates the sponsorship contract automatically.

</li>
<li>

[**Employees: redirect 3.5%**]({{< relref "/donate/persoane-fizice" >}})

Romanian employees can redirect **3.5% of their already-paid income tax** via Form 230. The dedicated page (in Romanian) pre-fills the official form.

</li>
</ul>

{{< callout type="important" >}}
**Status of the tax mechanisms:** deductible sponsorships and the 3.5% redirection become available once Asociatia OncoGuide is listed in the Romanian tax authority's non-profit registry (in progress). Direct donations are possible right now.
{{< /callout >}}

## Direct donation by bank transfer

Available to anyone -- individual or company, in Romania or abroad. The account details are below; the table is filled automatically from the association's official data.

**Good to know:** a plain donation carries no tax benefit. If you are a Romanian company or employee, the options above cost you effectively nothing, because you redirect taxes already owed.

## Where the money goes

Asociatia OncoGuide has a single statutory purpose: closing the gap between medical knowledge and the information available to cancer patients. Funds support keeping the site running, access to medical sources, and new guides in 6 languages.

For transparency: the association keeps a record of received donations, and our donor privacy policy is public: [donor privacy]({{< relref "/donate-privacy" >}}).
